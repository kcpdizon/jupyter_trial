## Virtual Chinatown Heritage Walking Tour

According to Gracie Plata, Tourism Relations Specialist II of the City Tourism Operations Office (CTOO), the introduction of Davao's four new tourism circuits are to highlight the city's multicultural roots. Among the four new circuits, two are centered around Davao's historical migrant communities: the Japanese and the Chinese. 

Launched in June 2026 under the new *Duaw Davao* Festival, the Chinatown Heritage Walking Tour welcomed over 200 participants across four consecutive weekends. *Duaw Davao* joins the city's major quarterly fesetivals: *Araw ng Dabaw* (founding anniversary) in March, *Duaw Davao* (visit Davao) in June, *Kadayawan* in August, and *Pasko Fiesta* in December. While the current route covered only one section of Chinatown, the strong turnout signals an interest in Davao's rich heritage. According to Plata, a full tour of the Chinatown area is currently under development, with a targeted launch for Chinese New Year 2027.
